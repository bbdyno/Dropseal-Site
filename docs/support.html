My capsule stays at 0%

Dropseal can only count activity Health has recorded after the capsule was sealed. Check that access is on in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Dropseal. Apple doesn't tell apps whether reading was allowed, so the app can't detect this for you.

Progress updates late

iOS wakes Dropseal for new Health data at most about once an hour, and Health data is unreadable while the iPhone is locked. Opening the app always measures right away. Background App Refresh must be on for background updates.

I deleted Health data — will my capsule seal again?

No. Once a capsule unseals it stays unsealed, and progress never goes backwards.
