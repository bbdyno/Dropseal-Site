Dropseal is a time capsule for your iPhone. Write a letter, add photos, and seal it behind an activity goal. It stays closed until you've walked, burned or trained your way there.

Scroll to walk ↓

How it works

1

Write

A letter to your future self, a promise, a photo of today. Text and photos, as much as you like.

2

Seal

Pick what breaks the seal — 50,000 steps, 3,000 kcal, 300 minutes of exercise. Only activity after sealing counts.

3

Move

Dropseal reads your progress from Apple Health. Your iPhone and Apple Watch both count, without double counting.

4

Open

As you move, the wax softens and runs. Reach the goal and the seal gives way — you'll get a notification if it happens while the app is closed.
